How to use this app
  1. Log work: type a client name (pick an existing one and their rate fills in), then the date, a short description and the time in hours and minutes. Press Add entry and the charge is worked out for you.
  2. Monthly: choose a month to see what you earned and what is still to invoice. After sending a client’s invoice, press Mark as invoiced in their box.
  3. Clients: add your real clients with their hourly rate, change a rate, or delete a client. Sample data is marked Sample. Delete it when you’re ready, or restore it any time to practise.
  4. Saving: everything is saved automatically in this browser on this device. If you delete something, it is gone.
